How The Tip-Off works

Every women's basketball game, the channel or app it's on, and which ones you can watch free. Here's how to use it.

Link copied

Get started in four steps

  1. See what's on today

    The featured game, live scores and today's schedule are right on the home page.

  2. Follow your teams

    Search any team in My Teams. Its next game, last result and news stay front and center.

  3. Tap "Where to watch"

    Every game lists its channels and apps, marked Free or Subscription needed.

  4. Filter for free games

    Use Filters to see only free games, or just the services you already have.

For creators

  • Share this page with your audience; it's a quick way to show them how to find any game.
  • Link to a specific game: open a game on The Tip-Off and tap Share for a link straight to its "Where to watch" page.
  • thetipoffguide.com always shows today's games first, so the same link works every day.
