How the lottery works

  • Two picks are drawn. Fourteen balls make 1,000 four-number combinations. The team holding the first combination drawn gets No. 1, then the drawing repeats for No. 2.
  • Picks 3 to 7 go to the remaining teams by record, worst record first.
  • Safety nets: Houston can't fall past No. 3, and Toronto is guaranteed a top-four pick.
  • Los Angeles has two entries: its own pick, plus Phoenix's pick from a trade.
