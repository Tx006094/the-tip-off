Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

The short version

  • You don't need an account to use The Tip-Off.
  • Your teams, services and settings are saved in your own browser, not on our servers.
  • If you sign up for our email, we keep your email address only to send it. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • We don't sell your information or use advertising cookies.

This policy explains how The Tip-Off (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use thetipoffguide.com (the “site”) or sign up for our email. It covers visitors in the United States and Canada.

1. Information saved on your device

To remember your choices between visits, the site saves a few settings in your browser's local storage. This information stays on your device. We can't see it, and it isn't sent to us.

What is savedWhy
Teams you add to My Teams fc-favsTo show your teams' games first
Services you select in My Viewing Options fc-servicesTo show games you can already watch
U.S. or Canada setting fc-regionTo show the right channels and services
Conference filter fc-confTo keep your schedule filter
Scores ticker paused or playing fc-ticker-pausedTo remember your ticker preference

You can delete these at any time by clearing your browser's site data for thetipoffguide.com. The site doesn't use cookies.

2. Our weekly email

If you sign up, we collect your email address and the date you signed up. We use it only to send our email about upcoming games and site updates. We may also see basic delivery information, such as whether an email was delivered, opened or clicked, so we can keep the email useful and stop sending to addresses that no longer work.

  • Consent. We send email only to people who sign up. We may ask you to confirm your address first.
  • Unsubscribing. Every email has an unsubscribe link. You can also email us to be removed. We process requests promptly.
  • Service provider. Your address is stored and sent through an email service provider that handles it for us under its own security and privacy obligations. It may store data outside your province or country, including in the United States.
  • Sponsors. An email may include a message from a sponsor. We never give sponsors your email address.

3. Information collected automatically

Hosting

The site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

Services the site loads

To show live schedules and team information, your browser connects directly to:

  • ESPN, for schedules, scores, headlines and team images
  • Google Fonts, for the site's typefaces

These companies receive your IP address and basic browser information when your browser loads their content. Their own privacy policies apply.

Analytics

We don't currently use analytics. If we add it, we'll use a privacy-friendly tool that doesn't use cookies or track you across sites, and we'll update this policy first.

4. Links to other sites

The site links to streaming services, TV providers, calendars and other websites. When you leave our site, that site's privacy policy applies. Calendar links send the game details (not your personal information) to Google Calendar or Outlook only when you choose to use them.

5. How we share information

We don't sell or rent your personal information, and we don't share it for targeted advertising. We share it only:

  • with service providers that help us run the site and email, as described above
  • if required by law, or to protect the site and its users
  • if the site is transferred to a new owner, who would be bound by this policy

6. How long we keep it

We keep your email address while you're subscribed. After you unsubscribe, we delete it or keep only what we need to make sure you aren't emailed again.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, which is generally just your email address. Depending on where you live, including Canada and some U.S. states, you may have additional rights under local privacy law. Email us and we'll respond within 30 days. You may also contact your local privacy regulator, such as the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

8. Security

The site uses HTTPS, and we collect as little information as possible. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we use reputable service providers and limit who can access subscriber information.

9. Children

The site is for a general audience. Our email is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has signed up, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your email address, we'll tell subscribers before it takes effect.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: privacy@thetipoffguide.com
