TheIXPodcast joins The Tip-Off

The home for where to watch women's basketball is now a home for the conversation around it.

NOVEMBER 13, 2026 — The Tip-Off is excited to announce that TheIXPodcast is officially joining the platform, bringing its coverage of women's basketball to a growing destination for fans of the game.

From the WNBA and NCAA women's college basketball to Unrivaled, UpShot, FIBA, and the Olympics, TheIXPodcast delivers news, analysis, player breakdowns, and conversations surrounding the biggest stories in women's hoops.

Every new episode will be featured directly on The Tip-Off, giving fans one place to discover when and where to watch their favorite teams and stay connected to the stories shaping the sport.

The partnership brings together two ways to experience women's basketball: knowing when and where the games are happening and staying connected to the conversations that make the sport worth following.

With TheIXPodcast now part of the experience, The Tip-Off continues its mission of making women's basketball easier to find, follow, and enjoy.
