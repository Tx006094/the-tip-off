TheIXPodcast joins The Tip-Off Guide

The home for where to watch women's basketball is now a home for the conversation around it.

NOVEMBER 13, 2026 — The Tip-Off Guide today announced that TheIXPodcast, the show covering the WNBA and NCAA women's basketball, is officially joining the site. Every new episode will stream on The Tip-Off Guide alongside the daily schedule, so fans can find tonight's games and the stories behind them in one place.

The move marks the next step for The Tip-Off Guide, growing from a schedule tool into a full women's basketball media destination. More shows, features and coverage are on the way.
