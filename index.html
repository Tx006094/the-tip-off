    How to watch women's basketball in 2026–27

    Women's basketball is spread across more than 40 channels and apps. Here's where each league airs, and how to watch without paying.

    WNBA

    Follow the W on ABC, ESPN, NBC, Peacock, Prime Video, CBS and ION. Catch out-of-market games with WNBA League Pass, and check local stations for coverage of your home team.

    Watch free: ABC, CBS, NBC and ION with an antenna, where available.

    NCAA Division I Women's Basketball

    From opening night to March Madness, find games on ESPN+, ESPN, ESPN2, ABC, FOX, FS1, CBS and conference networks like SEC Network, ACC Network and Big Ten Network. Follow the NCAA Tournament on ESPN's networks, with the national championship on ABC.

    Watch free: Select games on ABC, CBS, FOX and NBC with an antenna, plus free conference streams.

    Unrivaled

    Big stars. Fast-paced 3-on-3. Catch Unrivaled on TNT and truTV, or stream on HBO Max. Season three tips off January 8, 2027, with tour stops including Los Angeles and San Francisco.

    Watch free: No free options. A paid TV or streaming subscription is required.

    UpShot League

    Catch the Southeast's pro league on YouTube, the free Scripps Sports channel and local broadcast stations in its home markets.

    Watch free: Every game. No paid subscription needed.

    Olympic & FIBA

    Follow the international game on FIBA's Courtside 1891, with select games on YouTube and ESPN+. For Olympic basketball, tune in to NBC and Peacock. Next stop: Los Angeles 2028.

    Watch free: Select FIBA games on YouTube and Olympic coverage on NBC with an antenna.

    How to watch for free

    1. Get an antenna. A basic indoor antenna picks up ABC, CBS, NBC, FOX, ION and The CW in HD. That covers many marquee WNBA and college games.
    2. Use free streams. UpShot League games and many smaller-conference college games stream free on YouTube and conference sites.
    3. Time your free trials. Many streaming services offer a trial. Start one right before a big game or tournament.
    4. Filter by Free. Tap Free at the top of The Tip-Off to see only games you can watch without a subscription.
    5. Use what you already pay for. Tap My Viewing Options and tick the services you have, like Prime Video, Peacock or your cable package, to see every game you can already watch.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I watch women's basketball for free?

    Yes. Games on ABC, CBS, NBC, FOX, ION and The CW are free with a TV antenna. UpShot League games stream free on YouTube and the Scripps Sports channel, and several college conferences stream games free on their own networks. The Tip-Off marks every free option with a green Free label.

    How can I watch WNBA games without cable?

    WNBA games air on ABC, CBS, NBC and ION, which are free with an antenna, and stream on Prime Video, Peacock and ESPN's streaming plans. WNBA League Pass carries out-of-market games. ESPN and ESPN2 games need cable or a live-TV streaming service such as YouTube TV, Hulu + Live TV, Sling TV or Fubo.

    Which streaming service shows the most women's college basketball?

    ESPN+ carries more NCAA Division I women's basketball games than any other service. Conference networks such as SEC Network, ACC Network and Big Ten Network, plus B1G+, FloSports and FOX's channels, carry most of the rest.

    Where can I watch Unrivaled?

    Unrivaled games air on TNT and truTV and stream on HBO Max. There is no free option. The 2027 season tips off on January 8.

    How can I watch women's basketball in Canada?

    TSN is the main home for women's basketball in Canada. It carries every Toronto Tempo game plus other WNBA games, NCAA college games (mostly on TSN+) and FIBA and Canada Basketball games. Select Tempo games air free on CTV and stream on Crave, Prime Video has some exclusive WNBA games, and the Olympics are free on CBC and CBC Gem. Switch The Tip-Off to Canada to see Canadian channels for every game.

    How do I find games on the streaming services I already have?

    Tap My Viewing Options in the filter bar and tick what you have, such as an antenna, a cable or live-TV package, ESPN+, Prime Video, Peacock or HBO Max. The Tip-Off then shows only the games you can watch with those services, plus free streams, and marks them "Yours" everywhere on the site.

    Can I add women's basketball games to my calendar?

    Yes. Open any game and tap Add to calendar to save it to Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook with the channel listed. On a team's page you can add the whole schedule at once.

    Is The Tip-Off free to use?

    Yes. The Tip-Off is free and needs no account. It is an independent guide and is not affiliated with any league, team or broadcaster.
